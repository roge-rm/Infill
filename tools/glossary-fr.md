# French glossary

The words the French translation keeps to, so the app and the Help say the
same thing. Canadian French, as written in Quebec. The player is addressed as
"vous". Keep it plain and short, as in English: say what something does and
how to use it, no em dashes, no bold sentences, no summary lines. Button and
tab labels stay about as short as the English ones, since they have to fit.
