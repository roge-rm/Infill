# The town's story
> The chronicle, the graphs, then and now, and why lots aren't growing.

## The chronicle

Everything that happens to the town is kept in its chronicle: fires and floods, laws passed and repealed, the first of every kind of building, the town reaching 1,000 people and every milestone after, and each new era. Open it with **Chronicle** on the era card, or H on a keyboard.

It reads month by month, newest first, in the paper of the day: the town's gazette in the early years, a tabloid daily star from the Motor age, and a feed in the Infill era. **Headlines** shows the big news and **Everything** the lot. Tap a story with a place to go there.

## Then and now

As each era arrives, the town's picture is taken. The era card shows it beside the one taken when the era before began, so you can see how the town has grown.

## The graphs

**Graphs**, from the People window or G, shows the town over time: people, jobs, money, income and upkeep, crime, pollution, land value, carbon, leisure, health, those out of work, births, deaths, smog and approval. **Last 20 years** shows it month by month, and **Since** the year the town began shows every year back to the start. A dashed line marks each new era.

## Why not growing

The **Why not growing** map view, under **Town**, shows each zoned lot green where something can go up next and red where something holds it back. Inspect a red lot to see why, in the same words as the advice line. Lots already as built up as their zoning lets them show nothing.

The **Upset** view shows where clearings have upset people, **Empty homes** the homes standing empty, **Heritage** the buildings valued as heritage, with the ones that will be one day paler, and **Mood** how content each home is.
