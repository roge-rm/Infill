# A first town
> From an empty map to a town that grows by itself.

Infill starts in January 1900 with $20,000 and an empty piece of land. You lay the roads, zone the land and bring in power and water, and the town builds itself on what you give it. Over the years it fills in, and the eras ask more of it.

<!-- desktop: On a computer a click is a tap. The right or middle mouse button moves the map and the wheel zooms. The keys are in Settings. -->

## Starting

On the first screen, **New city** makes a new town. Pick a name, a map number and the land: how big a town is, whether there's sea, the climate, how much water and woods, a river, and whether it gets earthquakes. The preview shows the land as you change it. **Start** opens the town, paused.

**A region of towns** makes a set of towns side by side on one piece of land instead. See the Regions section.

## The first streets

1. Tap **Transport**, then **Road**, and drag a street in from the edge of the map. A road reaching the edge is how people and goods get in and out: when it asks, choose **Link it**.
2. Tap **Zones**, pick **Residential** and drag a block along the street. Lots grow up to three tiles from a road.
3. Zone some **Commercial** and **Industrial** too. Homes need jobs, and shops need people.
4. Press play.

Cottages, stores and workshops go up over the next few weeks. None of them need power or water yet.

## What the town asks for

The bars in the top strip are demand: up when the town wants more of a zone, down and faded when there's too much. Zone more of whatever's wanted.

The line under the strip says what's holding the town back, worst first: no road to a block, no power, no mains water, or too little money. Tap it to go to the place.

Houses want power before long and larger houses want mains water. Under **Utilities**, a **Coal power station** and a line of **Power line** to the zones bring power, and a **Pumping station** beside water with mains along the streets brings water. Drawing a road with **With pipes under it** on lays the mains as you go.

## Money

Each month the town collects taxes and pays upkeep on everything you've built. Tap the money in the strip for the budget. Taxes start at 7%. Raising them brings in more but slows growth, and lowering them does the reverse.

## Time and speed

Each month is one day and night on screen. The arrows in the strip change the speed, and the button beside them pauses. When a new era arrives the game pauses and says what it brings.

## Saving

The town saves itself at the start of every month and when you leave the app, and **Continue** on the first screen picks up from there. **Save** in the menu keeps a copy under the town's name.
