# The screen
> The strip, the tools, the map views, inspecting, undo and the keys.

## The strip

Along the top, from the left:

- The menu.
- Pause and the speed. Slow is twenty minutes a month, normal ten, fast two and a half.
- **Map view**, for the views below.
- The date. Tap it to see the era and what the next one needs.
- The weather and the temperature.
- The money, red when the town's in debt. Tap it for the budget.
- The population. Tap it for the People window.
- The demand bars, one for each zone.

On a narrow phone the strip is two lines, and undo and redo sit in it.

Under the strip is the advice line, and under that, news as it happens: a fire, a burst main, a new era. Tap either one to go to the place.

## Tools

The toolbar has six buttons: **Inspect**, **Bulldoze**, **Zones**, **Transport**, **Utilities** and **Services**. Then undo and redo.

A button opens its tray: tabs for the tools that share it, a line naming what's picked and its price, and the choices as tiles. Tap the line to fold the tray away, and it folds by itself once you've built something. Tapping the same button again does the same. Only what the year and era allow is shown.

With a tool picked, one finger builds and two fingers move and zoom the map. Lines like roads, track and pipes are dragged, and go along one way then the other. Buildings go where you lift your finger.

## Inspect

With **Inspect**, one finger moves the map and a tap opens a card on what's there: a picture, what it is, how it's doing, its figures, and what you can do with it. Buildings show their residents or jobs, staff, condition and needs. Roads show their traffic and wear, and crossings show how long it takes to get through. The strip along the bottom shows land value, crime and pollution.

## Map views

**Map view** opens the views, in four tabs:

- **Town**: land value, pollution, wealth, age, heat, what's in the ground, visitors, carbon and noise.
- **Utilities**: power, line load, phone and internet, water, runoff and garbage.
- **Services**: crime, theft, vice, rackets, police, fire cover, ladders, ambulances, schooling and health.
- **Transport**: traffic, railway, transit, goods, junctions, trips and reach.

Trips and reach work from the road you're inspecting: where its traffic comes from and goes, and how far you can drive from it.

## Undo

Undo takes back up to 100 things and gives back what they cost. It's for slips, so it only reaches back to the start of the month, and it can't take back something the town has built over since. Redo needs the money again.

## Keys

With a keyboard:

- Arrows or W, A, S and D move the map, and + and - zoom.
- 1 to 0 pick the tools: Inspect, Bulldoze, Road, Rail, Zone, Power, Water, Services, Transit and Traffic. Pressing one again steps through its kinds.
- B, G and P open the Budget, Graphs and People. V steps through the map views.
- Space pauses. Shift and 1, 2 or 3 set the speed.
- Esc closes what's open, then puts the tool down, then opens the menu.
- Ctrl and Z undoes, and Ctrl and Y or Ctrl, Shift and Z redoes.

The keys can be changed in Settings.

## Saving and loading

The town saves itself at the start of each month and whenever you leave the app, in one autosave. **Save** in the menu keeps it under its name, and a save with the same name is replaced. **Load** lists regions first, then towns with the autosave at the top. **Delete** asks for a second tap.
